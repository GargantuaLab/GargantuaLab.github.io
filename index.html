Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

We respect your privacy. Our apps are designed to collect as little personal information as possible.

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal information unless clearly required for a specific app feature.
  • Some apps may store data locally on your device.
  • If an app uses advertising, Google AdMob may collect data according to Google’s own advertising policies.
  • If an app uses Apple services such as Sign in with Apple or iCloud, Apple’s terms and privacy policies may apply.

For app-specific privacy details, please check the App Store privacy section for each app.

Security

We aim to keep our apps simple and secure.

  • We avoid unnecessary account creation where possible.
  • We prefer local storage for personal records when a server is not required.
  • When network communication is used, we aim to use secure HTTPS connections.
  • We do not ask for permissions that are not needed for the app’s core function.
